How it works

Three steps, then you're packing.

  1. Describe the trip

    Dates, city, budget, and who's coming. A sentence is enough — Llama asks follow-up questions if it needs them.

  2. Get a real itinerary

    Not a list of landmarks. An ordered day plan with travel times, opening hours, and gaps where gaps belong.

  3. Change your mind

    Drag a stop, drop a day, say "too much walking". The rest of the plan re-flows around it instead of breaking.
