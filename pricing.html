Questions

Before you subscribe

Can I cancel whenever I want?

Yes. Cancel from your account page and Premium runs to the end of the period you've already paid for. Nothing is clawed back and nothing auto-renews after that.

What happens to my trips if I downgrade?

They stay. You keep read access to every itinerary you've made — you just go back to editing one active trip at a time.

Which currencies can I pay in?

Llama prices in USD today. Your bank converts at its own rate; the charge on your statement shows as LLAMA LLC.

Do you offer annual billing?

Not yet. It's the request we get most, and it's next on the list.
